About the college

GCT Kamalia prepares students for careers in industry and entrepreneurship. The college focuses on practical skills, modern technical knowledge and professional conduct.

It is a government institution under the Technical Education and Vocational Training Authority (TEVTA), Government of Punjab.

Programs

The college offers diploma programs in engineering and technology fields.

Chemical Technology

Chemical processes, industrial operations and laboratory practice.

Civil Technology

Construction, surveying, structural design and project management.

Computer Information Technology

Programming, networking, database management, web development and IT systems.

Textile Technology

Three-year Diploma of Associate Engineering (DAE) in Textile Technology.

Admission

For admission queries, call or message the admission office. Admissions are open to deserving students who want to build technical skills.
