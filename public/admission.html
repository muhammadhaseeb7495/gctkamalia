Admission

Apply for a diploma program at Government College of Technology, Kamalia. Send an inquiry below or call the admission office.

Before you apply

Eligibility Matric (Science group) or equivalent, with the minimum passing marks required by PBTE. Age limit as per TEVTA/PBTE rules. Admission is on merit, with open merit and reserved quota seats as per government policy.

Fee, required documents and the last date can change each session. Please call the admission office to confirm them.
