About the college

Government College of Technology, Kamalia, a TEVTA Punjab institution on Chichawatni Road.

Who we are

GCT Kamalia prepares students for careers in industry and entrepreneurship. The college focuses on practical skills, modern technical knowledge and professional conduct.

It is a government institution under the Technical Education and Vocational Training Authority (TEVTA), Government of Punjab.
